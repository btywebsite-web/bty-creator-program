FAQ

Questions creators ask

Will I get free products when I join?

Not upon joining. We support you with RAW video content you can edit and customize in your own style. Free product opportunities may be given later, based on your sales, consistency and commitment.

How much can I earn?

Your income depends on your effort, content, checkouts, successful sales and consistency. You can earn through TikTok Shop, product links, content posting, live selling and other online selling strategies.

What happens after I submit the form?

Our Affiliate Team reviews your details and sends you a PDF with the full program details. Read it carefully, reply “I agree”, and we'll send your Content Kit.

Can I upload the RAW videos as they are?

Please don't. Edit them first with your own style, voice, hooks and captions. This makes your content more unique and engaging.

Do I need a minimum number of followers?

No. New affiliates, creators and online sellers are welcome. You do need to meet TikTok's own requirements to add products to your showcase, and our team can guide you.

How do I get paid?

Commissions are paid by TikTok Shop through your affiliate account once orders are completed. You can track them in the Affiliate Center.

Can I join if I'm outside the Philippines?

Yes. Apply and choose “Outside the Philippines”. BTY serves kababayans and OFWs abroad too.
